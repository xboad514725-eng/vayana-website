# VAYANA — Organic Marketing & Branding Execution Plan

**Approved direction (14 November 2026)**
- Audience: international travellers
- Language: multilingual
- Budget: organic only (no paid advertising)
- Brand promise: **TRAVEL YOUR WAY**
- Positioning: VAYANA helps guests shape their Luxor journey; it is not presented as a conventional travel agency.
- Customer wording: do not use “Share Tour” as a visible product label. Shared experiences remain fixed products, not optional customizations.

## 1. Brand foundation

### Core message
**Discover Luxor at your own pace. Travel your way with VAYANA.**

### Supporting messages
- Choose the experience, pace and dates that suit your trip.
- Combine tours, stays, flights and transportation in one request.
- Private experiences can be tailored within their stated rules.
- Shared experiences have fixed itineraries and inclusions.
- Every enquiry is reviewed; a request is not a confirmed booking until VAYANA confirms availability and details.

### Visual system
- Premium, minimal, editorial layout with generous white space and dark typography.
- Use the approved black suitcase emblem with the VAYANA wordmark.
- Keep the red, baby-blue, green and pink brand accents restrained.
- Prefer authentic Luxor photography: temples, Nile, balloon flights, feluccas and seasonal experiences.
- Avoid generic stock collages, fake booking interfaces and unapproved logo variations.

## 2. International and multilingual rollout

English remains the source language for the first release. Prepare translations as separate reviewed versions rather than relying on automatic browser translation.

**Recommended rollout order (to confirm against actual enquiries):**
1. English — source content
2. French
3. German
4. Spanish
5. Arabic

For every language:
- Translate page titles, navigation, forms, validation messages, confirmation messages, FAQs and AI hand-off text.
- Localize search phrases and travel terminology; do not translate keywords word-for-word.
- Add correct language, canonical and hreflang metadata only when a translated page is genuinely published.
- Keep dates unambiguous and phone fields international-format friendly.
- Keep all customer-facing prices and availability subject to confirmation.

## 3. Organic discovery channels

| Channel | Purpose | Organic execution |
|---|---|---|
| Website + SEO | Capture high-intent searches | Dedicated pages, useful travel guides, internal links, descriptive metadata and fast mobile layouts |
| Instagram | Visual discovery and trust | Reels, carousels, Stories, guest questions and seasonal highlights |
| TikTok | Reach new international audiences | Short vertical clips: temple details, Nile moments, balloon sunrise and practical travel tips |
| YouTube Shorts | Searchable evergreen video | Repurpose the strongest vertical videos with clear titles and captions |
| Google Business Profile | Local trust and discovery | Accurate profile, real photos, service descriptions, regular updates and review responses |
| Pinterest | Long-tail travel inspiration | Saveable itineraries, packing guides, temple highlights and seasonal trip boards |

No paid campaigns are included in this plan.

## 4. Content pillars

1. **Luxor, explained:** temples, tombs, history and what makes each place distinct.
2. **Travel your way:** private vs fixed shared experiences, trip pacing and combining services.
3. **Practical planning:** best time of day, what to bring, arrival planning and transport guidance.
4. **Experiences:** hot air balloon, felucca sailing, Nile views and local moments.
5. **Trust and service:** how requests work, what is confirmed, customer care and real operational details.
6. **Seasonal reasons to visit:** Christmas 2026, Winter 2026–27 and the 2 August 2027 total solar eclipse.

## 5. First 30 days

### Week 1 — Foundation
- Finalize English brand message and page descriptions.
- Review every page on mobile and confirm all enquiry routes.
- Complete the Travel Guide and connect it to Tours, Stays, Flights and Offers.
- Prepare a reusable set of authentic, rights-cleared Luxor images.
- Set up a simple content tracker and baseline website analytics.

### Week 2 — Useful discovery content
- Publish one Luxor planning article.
- Publish three short videos: Karnak details, a Nile/felucca moment and balloon sunrise.
- Publish one carousel explaining private and fixed shared experiences.
- Add internal links from each article to the relevant service page.

### Week 3 — Trust and conversion
- Publish a “How a VAYANA request works” explainer.
- Publish a transport and arrival-planning guide.
- Answer common questions in Stories and turn recurring questions into FAQs.
- Review form completion and failed submissions; fix friction before increasing content volume.

### Week 4 — Seasonal campaigns
- Publish Christmas in Luxor content focused on the actual destination, not generic snow imagery.
- Publish Winter travel-planning content.
- Begin a dedicated Solar Eclipse 2027 information series, clearly distinguishing confirmed details from arrangements still subject to confirmation.
- Review results and choose the next month’s topics from search and enquiry data.

## 6. Sustainable publishing rhythm

- 3 short videos per week.
- 2 feed posts or carousels per week.
- Stories on 4–5 days per week when there is useful material.
- 1 helpful website article every 1–2 weeks.
- 1 monthly review of search performance and enquiry quality.

Reuse content across Instagram, TikTok and YouTube Shorts, but adapt captions and titles to each platform.

## 7. Measurement

Track weekly:
- Organic website sessions and search clicks.
- Search impressions and queries by destination/service page.
- Enquiry starts, successful submissions and form errors.
- Qualified enquiries by service type and language.
- WhatsApp/contact clicks.
- Social reach, saves, shares and profile visits.
- Content that produces real enquiries, not only views.

Do not optimize for follower count alone. Prioritize qualified international enquiries and successful customer journeys.

## 9. Operating rules

- No paid advertising unless the organic approach is reviewed and a separate budget is approved.
- Never invent availability, prices, reviews, guest testimonials or operational claims.
- Never imply that an enquiry is a confirmed booking.
- Use only owned, licensed or permission-cleared photography.
- Keep the brand voice clear, warm, internationally understandable and consistent with **TRAVEL YOUR WAY**.
