A little preparation goes a long way

Before you travel.

Helpful basics to make your Luxor visit more comfortable.

01 / TIMING

Start early

Many outdoor archaeological sites are more comfortable to visit in the morning, especially during warmer months.

02 / WHAT TO BRING

Keep it light

Bring water, comfortable walking shoes, sun protection and clothing suitable for the day’s conditions.

03 / RESPECT

Travel thoughtfully

Follow site rules, respect local customs and ask before photographing people.

04 / BALLOON

Plan for sunrise

Balloon flights usually involve an early start and depend on weather and operator approval. Confirm details before travel.

Good to know

Your questions, answered.

Some useful details before sending your journey request.

Does a request confirm my booking?

No. Sending a request is not a confirmed booking. Vayana will review the details and respond with availability and a quotation.

Can I customize a private package?

Yes. Eligible private packages can be adjusted within the same bank area, subject to confirmation. Shared visits remain fixed.

Can I request a hot air balloon by itself?

Yes. You can request a standalone balloon experience without selecting a tour package. Flight availability and operation depend on weather and the operator.

Are guides and transportation included?

They depend on the option you select and the final quotation. Any included service will be confirmed before your booking.

Which languages can I request for a guide?

English-speaking and French-speaking guides can be requested. Availability is confirmed with your quotation.

How do I pay or cancel?

Payment methods, payment timing and cancellation terms will be shared with the quotation before you confirm any service.
